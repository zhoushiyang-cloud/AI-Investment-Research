## Moat Assessment

- **CUDA Ecosystem:** Dominant software platform for AI/ML training & inference — deep lock-in
- **Architecture Cadence:** Annual new GPU architecture releases (Hopper → Blackwell → Rubin)
- **Scale Advantages:** $30B+ R&D budget, TSMC allocation priority
- **Network Effects:** NVLink, InfiniBand, Spectrum-X networking stack

## Risks

1. **AI CapEx Cycle:** Hyperscaler spending may moderate if AI ROI disappoints
2. **Export Controls:** China restrictions limit ~20% of data center revenue
3. **Competition:** AMD MI300X, custom ASICs (Google TPU, Amazon Trainium, Microsoft Maia)
4. **Valuation Compression:** Premium multiple vulnerable to growth deceleration

## Catalysts

- Blackwell / B200 ramp driving FY2026 revenue
- Sovereign AI demand (national AI infrastructure builds)
- Automotive & robotics expansion (Omniverse, Isaac platform)
- Capital return: buybacks + potential dividend initiation
