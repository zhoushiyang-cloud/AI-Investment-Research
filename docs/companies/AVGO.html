Moat Assessment

  • Custom ASIC Dominance: Google TPU co-design partner (3nm next-gen), rumored OpenAI/Apple engagements
  • Networking Leadership: #1 in data center switching/routing silicon (Tomahawk 5, Jericho3-AI)
  • VMware Flywheel: Virtualization monopoly → private cloud stack (VCF) with high switching costs
  • M&A Playbook: Acquire, cut costs, harvest cash flow — proven across 10+ major deals

Risks

  1. Customer Concentration: Apple ~20% of semi revenue (wireless); Google is dominant ASIC customer
  2. VMware Integration: Channel backlash from per-core licensing shift, customer defections to Nutanix/Proxmox
  3. AI ASIC Durability: Will hyperscalers in-house ASIC design beyond current partnerships?
  4. Debt Load: $60B+ debt post-VMware; high rates pressure FCF

Catalysts

  • 3nm Google TPU v6 ramp → largest ASIC program yet
  • AI networking (Ethernet > InfiniBand thesis playing out at scale)
  • VMware VCF subscription transition boosting recurring revenue
  • Potential new ASIC customer announcements (Apple AI silicon?)
