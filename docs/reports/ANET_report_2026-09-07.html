ℹ️ Business Overview

Arista Networks designs and sells high-performance, software-driven Ethernet switching and routing platforms for large-scale data centers, cloud, and AI clusters. The company's core differentiator is the Extensible Operating System (EOS) — a single, consistent software image across all platforms — which simplifies operations and drives customer stickiness.

Key revenue segments (FY2025, estimated):

  • Data center switching (Cloud/AI): ~85-90% of revenue — 400G/800G platforms (7060X, 7800R series) for hyperscalers, cloud service providers, and large enterprises.

  • Routing (Edge/Spine): ~10% — 7280R/7500R platforms for WAN and data center interconnect.

  • Services & subscriptions: ~5-10% — EOS licensing, Arista CloudVision (network automation/telemetry), and professional services.

Competitive position: Arista is the #2 player in data center Ethernet switching (behind CSCO) but the clear leader in high-speed (400G+) AI/ML fabrics, with a 20%+ share of the overall data center switch market. Key competitive advantages:

  • Zero-touch provisioning and single-OS simplicity vs. fragmented legacy stacks.

  • Superior telemetry via CloudVision and DANZ monitoring.

  • Deep hyperscaler relationships (Microsoft, Meta, Oracle) and expanding enterprise traction.

  • Direct competition with NVDA's InfiniBand — Arista's Ethernet-based Spectrum-X alternative is gaining share as AI clusters scale beyond InfiniBand's limits.

    2. Top 2-3 hyperscalers (Microsoft, Meta, Oracle) likely represent >40% of revenue. Any slowdown in their AI capex or shift to in-house switching (e.g., Meta's open networking) would materially impact growth. FY2025 AR growth of 65% suggests heavy concentration.

  1. Valuation Compression Risk #medium

    2. At 61x trailing earnings, any guidance miss or macro tech selloff could trigger 20-30% de-rating. The stock fell from $214 to $181 (-15%) in the past 2 weeks on no company-specific news — sentiment is fragile.
