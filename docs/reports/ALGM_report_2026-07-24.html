ℹ️ Business Overview

Allegro MicroSystems (ALGM) is a fabless semiconductor company specializing in power management and sensor ICs for automotive and industrial markets. Key segments (estimated from revenue composition):

  • Automotive (~70% of revenue): Magnetic sensors, motor drivers, and power ICs for ADAS, EV powertrains, and chassis safety systems.

  • Industrial (~20%): Factory automation, renewable energy, and data center power management.

  • Other (~10%): Consumer and enterprise applications.

Competitive Position: ALGM holds a strong niche in high-reliability automotive magnetic sensors, competing with INFY (Infineon), NXPI (NXP), and STM (STMicroelectronics). Its key differentiator is proprietary GaN (gallium nitride) power IC technology for high-efficiency power conversion, which is gaining traction in EV and data center markets. However, the company lacks the scale and diversification of larger peers, making it highly sensitive to automotive end-market cycles.
