ℹ️ Business Overview

Palantir Technologies (PLTR) is a data analytics and AI platform company, primarily serving U.S. government agencies (defense, intelligence) and large commercial enterprises. The company’s core product is the Foundry operating system, now augmented by the AIP (Artificial Intelligence Platform) which enables large language model integration into enterprise workflows.

Key Revenue Segments (FY2025):

  • Government (U.S. + Allied): ~55-60% of revenue. Driven by long-term contracts with the DoD, CIA, and allied nations. High visibility, multi-year deals.

  • Commercial (U.S. + International): ~40-45% of revenue. The fastest-growing segment, fueled by AIP boot camps converting to large-scale deployments.

Competitive Position: Palantir holds a unique moat in highly sensitive, mission-critical government work where data security and provenance are paramount. In commercial, it competes with MSFT (Azure/AI), SNOW (data cloud), and custom AI solutions from NVDA-powered startups, but Palantir’s advantage lies in its ontology layer and ability to operationalize AI on messy, real-world data.
