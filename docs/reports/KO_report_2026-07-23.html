📋 Executive Summary

  • Quality Staple at a Premium: Coca-Cola (KO) is a world-class consumer defensive franchise with a 61.7% gross margin and a 27.8% net margin, but the current P/E of 25.6x and EV/EBITDA of 20.0x are elevated relative to historical averages.

  • Strong Cash Generation: TTM operating cash flow is $7.4B, supporting a $2.08 annual dividend (2.55% yield) and a 80% payout ratio, though FCF yield is a modest 3.58%.

  • Leverage is Manageable: Net debt-to-EBITDA of 1.73x is comfortable for a defensive business, but total debt-to-equity of 1.3x warrants monitoring given rising interest rates.

  • Growth is Muted: Revenue growth is low-single-digit organic; the stock's valuation relies on multiple expansion or stable yield, not earnings acceleration. Consensus target of $88 implies ~8% upside.

ℹ️ Business Overview

The Coca-Cola Company is the world's largest non-alcoholic beverage company, operating a global franchise system. It manufactures and sells concentrates, syrups, and finished beverages under iconic brands including Coca-Cola, Diet Coke, Sprite, Fanta, Dasani, Powerade, Minute Maid, and fairlife.

  • Segment Structure: The company reports through geographic segments (North America, Latin America, Europe/Middle East/Africa, Asia Pacific) and global ventures (Costa Coffee, BODYARMOR).

  • Revenue Model: ~60% of revenue comes from concentrate sales to independent bottling partners (asset-light, high-margin). The remaining ~40% comes from finished product sales through company-owned or consolidated bottling operations.

  • Competitive Position: Unmatched brand equity, distribution reach in over 200 countries, and a moat built on trademark portfolio and bottler relationships. Key competitors include PEP (PepsiCo), KDP (Keurig Dr Pepper), and MNST (Monster Beverage).

  • Key Metrics: TTM revenue of $47.9B, market cap of $351B, and 65,900 employees.

📊 Financial Analysis

  • Revenue & Profitability: TTM revenue is $47.9B, up from $47.1B in FY2024. Gross profit margin is a robust 61.7%, while operating margin is 29.3% and net margin is 27.8%. This reflects strong pricing power and an asset-light concentrate model.

  • Cash Flow: TTM operating cash flow is $7.41B. Capital expenditures are $2.11B (capex-to-revenue of 4.2%), yielding TTM free cash flow of $5.30B. The FCF yield is a low 3.58%.

  • Balance Sheet: Total assets of $104.8B, with goodwill and intangibles of $28.0B (26.7% of assets). Total debt is $45.5B, with net debt of $35.2B. The current ratio is 1.36x, and the quick ratio is 1.15x, indicating adequate liquidity.

  • Returns: Return on equity (ROE) is an exceptional 43.6%, driven by high leverage and share buybacks. Return on invested capital (ROIC) is 13.9%, demonstrating efficient capital deployment.

  • Dividend: Annual dividend of $2.08/share, yielding 2.55%. The dividend payout ratio is 80.1%, which is high but sustainable given the stable cash flow.

ℹ️ Valuation Assessment

  • Current Multiples: P/E of 25.6x, EV/EBITDA of 20.0x, P/S of 7.1x, and P/FCF of 27.9x. These are premium multiples for a low-growth defensive stock.

  • Peer Comparison: KO trades at a premium to PEP (P/E ~22x) and KDP (P/E ~19x), but at a discount to MNST (P/E ~30x). The premium vs. PEP is justified by KO's higher margins and stronger brand portfolio.

  • Historical Context: KO's 5-year average P/E is ~24x. The current 25.6x is slightly above the historical mean, suggesting limited upside from multiple expansion.

  • Analyst Consensus: Target high of $98, low of $83, consensus of $88.00, implying ~8% upside from the current price of $81.60. The PEG ratio of 0.93x suggests the P/E is justified by near-term growth, but the forward PEG of 3.86x indicates slower growth beyond the current year.

  • Conclusion: Valuation is fair but not compelling. The stock is a "bond proxy" with a 2.55% yield and modest growth, not a value play.

⚠️ Risks

  1. Currency Headwinds & Emerging Market Exposure #critical

    2. KO generates ~60% of revenue outside the US. A strengthening US dollar (DXY) directly pressures reported revenue and earnings. The company has significant exposure to volatile currencies in Latin America and Asia.

  2. Input Cost Inflation & Supply Chain #medium

    3. While KO has pricing power, sustained inflation in aluminum, PET plastic, and sugar could compress margins. The capex-to-depreciation ratio of 1.98x indicates higher maintenance spending.

  3. Shifting Consumer Preferences #medium

    4. Long-term trend away from sugary sodas toward healthier options (water, functional beverages). KO is adapting (e.g., fairlife, Topo Chico), but core sparkling soft drinks still dominate revenue.

  4. High Valuation & Low Growth #medium

    5. With revenue growth of ~2-3% and a P/E of 25.6x, the stock is vulnerable to multiple compression if interest rates rise or if the company delivers a growth miss.

  5. Bottler Disruption #low

    6. The franchise model relies on independent bottlers. Any financial distress or operational issues at major bottlers (e.g., KOF, CCEP) could disrupt distribution.

💡 Catalysts

  1. Innovation & Premiumization #near-term

    2. Continued success of higher-margin products like fairlife (dairy), Costa Coffee, and Topo Chico (hard seltzer) could accelerate revenue growth and margin expansion.

  2. Share Buybacks #near-term

    3. KO repurchased $746M in stock in FY2025. An acceleration of buybacks at current prices would be accretive to EPS, given the strong cash flow.

  3. Easing of Currency Headwinds #medium-term

    4. If the US dollar weakens, KO would see a tailwind to reported earnings, potentially driving consensus estimates higher.

  4. Dividend Increase #near-term

    5. KO has raised its dividend for 63 consecutive years. An above-consensus increase in February 2027 would reinforce the income thesis and attract yield-oriented investors.

💬 Recommendation

HOLD (Low Conviction)

  • 12-Month Price Target: $86.00 (based on 26x P/E on consensus FY2027 EPS of $3.30)

  • Thesis: Coca-Cola is a high-quality defensive business with a durable moat and reliable income. However, at 25.6x earnings with low-single-digit organic growth, the risk/reward is balanced. The stock offers a ~2.5% dividend yield and ~5% price appreciation potential, for a total return of ~7.5%. This is adequate for a defensive holding but not compelling for total return.
