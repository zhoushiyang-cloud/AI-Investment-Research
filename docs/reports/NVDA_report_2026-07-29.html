  • Exceptional Profitability: Operating income surged to $130.4B (60.4% margin) in FY2026, up from $81.5B (62.5% margin) in FY2025, showcasing immense operating leverage.

ℹ️ Business Overview

NVIDIA is the preeminent designer of graphics processing units (GPUs) and accelerated computing platforms. Its competitive position is fortified by the CUDA software ecosystem, which creates high switching costs for developers.

  • Compute & Networking (Data Center): The dominant segment, driving the vast majority of revenue. It includes Hopper and Blackwell architecture GPUs, networking (InfiniBand/Ethernet), and AI enterprise software. Revenue share is estimated at >85% of total revenue.

  • Graphics (Gaming): A mature but profitable segment for GeForce GPUs. Revenue share is estimated at <10%.

  • Professional Visualization & Automotive: Smaller segments focused on enterprise graphics and autonomous driving solutions. Combined revenue share is estimated at <5%.

📊 Financial Analysis

  • Revenue Growth: Total revenue exploded from $60.9B (FY2024) to $130.5B (FY2025) to $216.0B (FY2026) , a 3.5x increase in two years. This is a historic ramp.

  • Margins: Gross margin expanded to 71.1% in FY2026 (from 72.7% in FY2025). Operating margin is a staggering 60.4% , reflecting the asset-light model and pricing power. R&D spending of $18.5B is significant but well-leveraged.

  • Cash Flow: Operating cash flow was $102.7B in FY2026, up from $64.1B in FY2025. Free cash flow (OCF - CapEx) was approximately $96.7B.

  • Balance Sheet: The company is fortress-like. Total assets are $206.8B with $10.6B in cash and $38.5B in short-term investments. Total debt is only $8.5B ($7.5B long-term + $1.0B current). Total equity is $157.3B.

  • Capital Allocation: The company returned $40.1B to shareholders via buybacks in FY2026, demonstrating confidence in its intrinsic value.

ℹ️ Valuation Assessment

  • P/E (Trailing): Based on the current price of ~$208.76 and diluted EPS of $4.90, the trailing P/E is ~42.6x.

  • EV/EBITDA: Enterprise Value (EV) is approximately $2.08T (Market Cap ~$5.2T - Cash & Investments ~$49.1B). EBITDA is ~$133.2B (Operating Income + D&A). This yields an EV/EBITDA of ~15.6x.

  • Context: These multiples are elevated vs. the broader market (S&P 500 ~22x P/E) and NVDA's own 5-year average (~35x P/E). However, given the 65%+ revenue growth and 60%+ operating margins, the PEG ratio is below 1.0x, suggesting the premium is justified by the growth rate. The valuation is pricing in a multi-year AI capex cycle.

⚠️ Risks

  1. Hyperscaler Capex Slowdown #critical

    2. A significant portion of revenue comes from a few large customers (Microsoft, Amazon, Google, Meta). Any signal of reduced AI spending plans would severely impact NVDA's growth trajectory and likely compress its valuation multiple.

  2. Geopolitical / Export Controls #critical

    3. Escalating US-China tensions could lead to further restrictions on chip exports. While NVDA has created compliant chips, a total ban on sales to China or key regions would remove a meaningful growth vector and disrupt supply chains.

  3. Competitive Threat #medium

    4. Custom ASIC chips from AVGO and MRVL (e.g., Google TPU, AWS Trainium) are gaining traction for inference workloads. While NVDA leads in training, a shift in the AI workload mix towards custom silicon could erode market share over the next 2-3 years.

  4. Supply Chain Concentration #medium

    5. NVDA relies almost exclusively on TSMC for advanced chip fabrication. Any disruption at TSMC (geopolitical, natural disaster) would halt NVDA's production, impacting revenue and customer relationships.

💡 Catalysts

  • Blackwell Ultra / Rubin Architecture Launch #near-term

    • The next-generation GPU architectures are expected to drive another product cycle, compelling hyperscalers to upgrade and expand capacity, sustaining revenue growth through FY2027.

  • Enterprise AI Adoption #medium-term

    • As AI moves beyond cloud hyperscalers to enterprise IT, demand for NVDA's full-stack solutions (hardware + software) could broaden the customer base and reduce concentration risk.

  • New Product Categories (Robotics/AV) #medium-term

    • The "physical AI" opportunity (autonomous vehicles, humanoid robots, industrial automation) represents a massive TAM expansion beyond data centers, potentially opening a new multi-billion dollar revenue stream.
