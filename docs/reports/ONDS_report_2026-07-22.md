> [!info] Business Overview
> Ondas Holdings Inc. operates through two primary segments, targeting mission-critical industrial and government applications.
> - **Ondas Networks:** Provides the FullMAX software-defined radio (SDR) platform for wide-area, private broadband communications. This is the foundational connectivity layer for critical infrastructure like railways, energy grids, and utilities. Revenue contribution is not explicitly segmented in the data, but the technology is a key differentiator.
> - **American Robotics:** Offers a fully automated drone ecosystem (Scout Drone, ScoutBase hub, ScoutView software) for industrial data collection and inspection. This segment is the primary driver of recent revenue growth, targeting agriculture, mining, and infrastructure.
> - **Competitive Position:** ONDS competes in fragmented markets against larger players like [[AAOI]] (optical components) and niche drone operators. Its competitive advantage lies in the integration of its proprietary FullMAX wireless technology with its drone platform, offering a secure, end-to-end solution. The company serves a diverse range of sectors including railway, energy, mining, and agriculture.

>    Despite the large cash balance, operating cash burn was -$38.7M in FY2025. While the runway is long, the company must demonstrate a clear trajectory toward positive free cash flow. Continued losses without a clear path to profitability will pressure the stock.
