ℹ️ Business Overview

Allegro MicroSystems (ALGM) designs and sells analog and mixed-signal semiconductor solutions, primarily for automotive (approx. 70%+ of revenue) and industrial end-markets. Core products include magnetic sensors, current sensors, and power ICs for electric vehicles (EVs), ADAS, and industrial automation.

Competitive Position: ALGM competes with ADI, TXHN, and ON in the automotive sensing space. Its moat lies in proprietary magnetic sensor technology and high-barrier automotive qualification (AEC-Q100), but it lacks the scale of larger peers.

Key Segment Mix (based on revenue trajectory):

  • Automotive: Dominant driver; EV content growth (xEV) is the primary growth vector.

  • Industrial & Other: Includes alternative energy, data center, and general industrial—growing but smaller.

FY2026 Revenue: $890.1M (+22.8% YoY), recovering from the FY2025 trough of $725.0M (-30.9% from FY2024's $1,049.4M). The FY2024–FY2025 decline was a cyclical downturn; FY2026 shows a strong V-shaped recovery.
