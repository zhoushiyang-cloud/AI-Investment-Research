ℹ️ Business Overview

Applied Optoelectronics (AAOI) designs and manufactures fiber-optic networking products, including optical transceivers, laser components, and turn-key systems. Founded in 1997 and headquartered in Sugar Land, TX, the company serves three primary end-markets:

  • Internet data center operators (largest and fastest-growing segment) — 800G/1.6T optical modules for AI/ML clusters

  • Cable TV (CATV) equipment manufacturers — legacy headend and node infrastructure

  • Telecom/ISP providers — fiber-to-the-home and long-haul transport gear

Competitive positioning: AAOI is a second-tier optical module supplier competing against COHR (Coherent), LITE (Lumentum), AVGO (Broadcom), and MRVL (Marvell) in the high-speed datacom market. Its key differentiator is vertical integration — in-house laser and photodetector manufacturing — which provides cost advantages and supply chain control. The company has won design-ins at major U.S. hyperscalers (Microsoft, Meta, Amazon) for 800G modules, which drove the FY2025 revenue inflection.

Revenue concentration risk: The top customers account for a disproportionate share of revenue (data not available in this filing, but historically >50% from 2-3 customers). The recent stock surge on August 4, 2026 suggests a new or expanded customer contract was announced, but specific details are not in the provided data.
