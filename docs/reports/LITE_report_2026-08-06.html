ℹ️ Business Overview

Lumentum Holdings Inc. is a global leader in optical and photonic products, headquartered in San Jose, CA, with 10,562 employees. The company operates through two principal segments:

Optical Communications (OpComms) — the dominant revenue driver, supplying components, modules, and subsystems for high-capacity fiber optic networks. Key products include tunable transponders, transceivers, ROADMs, optical amplifiers, VCSELs, and 3D sensing laser illumination sources. Customers span telecom, datacom, consumer, and industrial markets. This segment is the primary beneficiary of AI datacenter buildouts requiring high-speed optical interconnects.

Commercial Lasers — produces diode-pumped solid-state, fiber, diode, direct-diode, and gas lasers for OEM applications across sheet metal processing, biotechnology, graphics/imaging, remote sensing, and precision machining.

Competitive positioning: LITE competes with AVGO (Broadcom) in datacom optics, FN (Fabrinet) as a manufacturing partner, and FFIV (F5) in adjacent networking infrastructure. The company's proprietary VCSEL and ROADM technologies provide differentiation, but competition from Chinese optical vendors and vertically-integrated hyperscaler designs remains intense.

Recent strategic moves: The company has pivoted aggressively toward AI/cloud datacenter opportunities, evidenced by the FY25 revenue rebound (+21% YoY) after a challenging FY24. The FY24 loss was driven by a $700.9M acquisition (likely Cloud Light) and restructuring charges.
