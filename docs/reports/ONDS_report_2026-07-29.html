ℹ️ Business Overview

Ondas Holdings operates in the autonomous drone and data intelligence sector, providing full-stack hardware and software solutions for industrial, defense, and public safety applications. The company’s key segments include:

  • Autonomous Drone Systems (estimated ~70% of FY2025 revenue): Includes the Ondas Autonomous Systems (OAS) platform, which provides drone-in-a-box solutions for perimeter security, inspection, and surveillance.

  • Data & Software Solutions (~30% of FY2025 revenue): Includes the Ondas Networks division, offering secure, wide-area wireless data networks for critical infrastructure.

  • Competitive Position: ONDS competes with AeroVironment, Skydio, and DJI in the drone space, but differentiates through its proprietary "Full Spectrum" software and hardware integration. The company has secured contracts with government and industrial clients, but faces intense competition from larger, better-capitalized players.

  • Key Clients: Data not available for specific customer concentration, but the company’s revenue growth suggests initial traction with defense and industrial end-markets.
