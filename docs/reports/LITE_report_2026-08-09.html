ℹ️ Business Overview

Lumentum Holdings Inc. is a global leader in optical and photonic products, headquartered in San Jose, CA. The company operates through two principal segments:

  • Optical Communications (OpComms) — The dominant segment (~85-90% of revenue), supplying components, modules, and subsystems for high-capacity fiber optic networks. Key products include tunable transceivers, ROADMs, optical amplifiers, VCSELs, and 3D sensing laser illumination sources. Customers span telecom, datacom, consumer, and industrial markets.

  • Commercial Lasers (~10-15% of revenue) — Produces diode-pumped solid-state, fiber, diode, and gas lasers for OEM applications in sheet metal processing, biotechnology, precision machining, and remote sensing.

Competitive Position: LITE is a top-tier player in optical components, competing directly with AVGO (Broadcom) in datacom optics and COHR (Coherent) in telecom/lasers. The company's ROADM and tunable laser franchise is best-in-class, and its VCSEL/3D sensing technology has strategic importance for consumer electronics (Apple supply chain).

Key Strategic Shift: Under CEO Michael Hurlston, LITE has pivoted aggressively toward AI datacenter optics, positioning itself as a key enabler of next-generation AI clusters. The company's recent product wins in 800G and 1.6T transceivers are the primary driver of the stock's re-rating.

Employees: 10,562 full-time staff. Beta of 1.48 indicates high cyclicality and sensitivity to tech sentiment.
