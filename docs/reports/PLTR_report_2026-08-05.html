ℹ️ Business Overview

Palantir Technologies (PLTR) is a data analytics and AI software company specializing in integrating, managing, and analyzing disparate data sources for decision-making. The company operates two primary segments:

  • Government: Includes defense, intelligence, and public sector clients (historically ~55-60% of revenue). This segment provides stable, long-term contracts with high switching costs.

  • Commercial: The fastest-growing segment, driven by the Artificial Intelligence Platform (AIP). U.S. commercial revenue has become the key growth engine, with customer counts expanding rapidly as enterprises adopt AI solutions.

Competitive Position: Palantir competes with NVDA (hardware/AI infrastructure), AVGO (custom silicon), and cloud hyperscalers, but differentiates through its ontology layer and "human-in-the-loop" decision-making software. The company's moat lies in its data integration capabilities and the difficulty of migrating away from deeply embedded deployments. The AIP launch in 2023 has transformed the growth narrative, moving Palantir from a "picks-and-shovels" data integrator to a direct AI application layer provider.
