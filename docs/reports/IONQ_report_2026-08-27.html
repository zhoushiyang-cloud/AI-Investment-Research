ℹ️ Business Overview

IonQ, Inc. designs and builds general-purpose quantum computing systems, primarily accessed via cloud platforms. The company's core product is its trapped-ion quantum computers, currently offering access to systems with up to 20+ qubits through:

  • Amazon Braket (AMZN) — primary distribution channel

  • Microsoft Azure Quantum (MSFT) — enterprise reach

  • Google Cloud Marketplace (GOOGL) — additional distribution

  • Direct cloud service — proprietary access for enterprise customers

Revenue model: Usage-based cloud access, consulting, and managed services. FY2025 revenue of $130.0M represents a 202% YoY increase from $43.1M in FY2024.

Competitive positioning: IonQ is widely considered a technology leader in trapped-ion quantum computing, competing with IBM (superconducting), GOOGL (superconducting), and pure-play peers QBTS (D-Wave, annealing) and RGTI (Rigetti, superconducting). IonQ's advantage lies in higher qubit fidelity and error rates, though the industry remains pre-commercial.

Key strategic moves: The FY2025 balance sheet shows $1.96B in goodwill and $767M in intangible assets, indicating significant M&A activity (likely the acquisition of Oxford Ionics and other quantum startups) to consolidate technology and talent. Headcount stands at 1,132 employees, up from ~300 in FY2023.
