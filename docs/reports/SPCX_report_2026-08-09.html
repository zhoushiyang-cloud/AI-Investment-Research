ℹ️ Business Overview

Space Exploration Technologies Corp. (SPCX) is the world's leading private space company, founded in 2002 and headquartered in Starbase, Texas, with 22,000 employees. The company operates across three core segments:

  • Launch Services (~45% of revenue) — Falcon 9 and Falcon Heavy provide commercial, civil, and national security launch capabilities. The company has effectively monopolized the US orbital launch market, with a near-cadence of weekly launches and a reusable first-stage architecture that undercuts competitors on cost per kilogram.

  • Starlink (~40% of revenue) — The satellite broadband constellation is the company's cash cow, serving consumer, enterprise, and maritime/aero markets across the US, Ireland, and Canada. With thousands of operational satellites, Starlink has become the dominant LEO broadband provider and is expanding into direct-to-cell and enterprise government contracts.

  • Starship & Dragon (~15% of revenue) — Dragon provides crew and cargo services to the ISS and commercial LEO destinations. Starship, currently in suborbital/orbital flight testing, is the long-term growth engine for deep-space missions (Moon, Mars) and heavy-lift payloads. Starshield serves government sensing, communications, and satellite bus needs.

Competitive position: SPCX has no credible near-term US competitor in launch (BA and LMT are decades behind on reusability), and Starlink faces only nascent competition from AMZN's Project Kuiper. The company's vertical integration — from manufacturing to launch to satellite operations — creates an unmatched cost and speed advantage.
