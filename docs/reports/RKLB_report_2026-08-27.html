ℹ️ Business Overview

Rocket Lab USA, Inc. is a vertically integrated aerospace company headquartered in Long Beach, CA, operating across two primary segments:

1. Launch Services (Electron)

  • The Electron small-lift vehicle is the company's workhorse, with a proven track record of commercial and government launches. It is the second-most launched U.S. rocket annually (behind SpaceX's Falcon 9).

  • Electron generates recurring revenue from a diverse customer base including commercial constellations, NASA, and the DoD.

2. Space Systems (Photon, Components, Mission Management)

  • Photon satellite platforms provide end-to-end spacecraft design, manufacturing, and on-orbit operations.

  • The company also sells reaction wheels, star trackers, solar panels, and other components to third-party satellite manufacturers — a high-margin, scalable business.

  • Constellation management services (launch + spacecraft + operations) are a key differentiator, allowing RKLB to capture full mission value.

Competitive Position:

  • RKLB is the clear #2 in Western launch, with a monopolistic position in the small-launch segment (no direct competitor has achieved comparable flight cadence).

  • The Neutron medium-lift vehicle (8-ton payload class) is the next major growth catalyst, targeting the megaconstellation and national security launch markets.

  • The company serves commercial, civil (NASA), and defense (DoD) customers, with defense spending a growing tailwind.
