ℹ️ Business Overview

Ondas Holdings Inc. operates through two primary segments, targeting mission-critical industrial and government applications.

  • Ondas Networks: Provides the FullMAX software-defined radio (SDR) platform for wide-area, private broadband communications. This is the foundational connectivity layer for critical infrastructure like railways, energy grids, and utilities. Revenue contribution is not explicitly segmented in the data, but the technology is a key differentiator.

  • American Robotics: Offers a fully automated drone ecosystem (Scout Drone, ScoutBase hub, ScoutView software) for industrial data collection and inspection. This segment is the primary driver of recent revenue growth, targeting agriculture, mining, and infrastructure.

  • Competitive Position: ONDS competes in fragmented markets against larger players like AAOI (optical components) and niche drone operators. Its competitive advantage lies in the integration of its proprietary FullMAX wireless technology with its drone platform, offering a secure, end-to-end solution. The company serves a diverse range of sectors including railway, energy, mining, and agriculture.

📊 Financial Analysis

  • Revenue: FY2025 total revenue was $50.7M, a dramatic increase from $7.2M in FY2024 and $15.7M in FY2023. This inflection suggests initial commercial traction for the drone and network solutions.

  • Margins: Gross profit improved to $20.2M (39.7% margin) in FY2025, up from a near-zero $0.3M (4.8% margin) in FY2024. This is a positive sign of scaling, but still well below mature peers.

  • Operating Expenses: Total operating expenses surged to $78.5M in FY2025, driven by $20.9M in R&D and $13.2M in SG&A. This heavy investment is necessary to capture the market but is the primary driver of losses.

  • Profitability: Net loss attributable to common shareholders was -$137.2M in FY2025, compared to -$42.4M in FY2024. The loss per share (basic) was -$0.62, an improvement from -$0.61 in FY2024 despite the larger net loss, due to dilution.

  • Cash Flow & Balance Sheet: Operating cash flow was -$38.7M in FY2025, an improvement from -$33.5M in FY2024. The company ended FY2025 with $550.7M in cash and equivalents against $0 in long-term debt, providing a substantial runway. However, this cash position was entirely funded by $829.5M in equity issuance, causing massive dilution.
