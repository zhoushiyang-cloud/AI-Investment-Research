ℹ️ Business Overview

Palantir Technologies Inc. engineers and deploys advanced software platforms primarily for the intelligence community, supporting counterterrorism investigations and operations across the United States, the United Kingdom, and internationally. Founded in 2003 and headquartered in Denver, Colorado, the company operates through four core product lines:

  • Palantir Gotham — The flagship intelligence platform that enables users to uncover hidden patterns within diverse datasets, from signals intelligence to confidential informant reports. It facilitates seamless handover between analysts and operational personnel for real-world threat response.

  • Palantir Foundry — A central data operating system that revolutionizes organizational operations, allowing individual users to integrate and analyze essential data in one cohesive environment. This is the primary commercial growth driver.

  • Palantir Apollo — A software delivery solution for deploying applications and updates across an enterprise, enabling clients to deploy software in virtually any setting (cloud, on-premise, edge).

  • Palantir Artificial Intelligence Platform (AIP) — Offers unified access to open-source, self-hosted, and commercial large language models (LLMs). AIP excels at transforming structured and unstructured data into LLM-understandable objects, converting organizational actions and processes into practical tools for human operators and LLM-driven agents.

Competitive Position: Palantir competes in the AI/analytics software space against CRM (Salesforce), PANW (Palo Alto Networks), CRWD (CrowdStrike), and PATH (UiPath). Its key differentiator is the deep integration with government/intelligence workflows (Gotham) and the enterprise data fabric approach (Foundry + AIP). With only 4,401 employees and a market cap of $407.6B, the company generates $1.02M in revenue per employee — among the highest in the sector. The company has no direct comparable in the pure-play AI software space, which partially justifies its premium valuation.
