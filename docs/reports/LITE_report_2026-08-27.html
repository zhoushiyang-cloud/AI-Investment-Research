ℹ️ Business Overview

Lumentum Holdings Inc. is a global leader in optical and photonic products, headquartered in San Jose, CA. The company operates through two principal segments:

  • Optical Communications (OpComms) — the dominant revenue driver (est. ~85-90% of revenue), supplying components, modules, and subsystems for high-capacity fiber optic networks. Key products include tunable transceivers, ROADMs, optical amplifiers, VCSELs, and 3D sensing laser illumination. This segment serves telecom, datacom, consumer, and industrial markets — and is the primary beneficiary of AI datacenter buildouts requiring high-speed optical interconnects.

  • Commercial Lasers — produces diode-pumped solid-state, fiber, diode, direct-diode, and gas lasers for OEM applications across sheet metal processing, manufacturing, biotech, graphics/imaging, and precision machining. This is a smaller, more mature segment.

Competitive positioning: LITE competes with COHR (Coherent), AVGO (Broadcom), and FN (Fabrinet) in optical components. The company's differentiation lies in its vertically integrated photonic capabilities, particularly in tunable lasers and ROADM technology, which are critical for AI cluster networking. The FY2026 revenue surge to $3.01B (from $1.65B in FY2025) signals significant share gains in AI optical modules.

Leadership: CEO Michael E. Hurlston has steered the company through a major product cycle shift toward AI-optimized optics. With 13,757 employees, the company has scaled operations significantly to meet demand.
