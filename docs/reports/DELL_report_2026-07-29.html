ℹ️ Business Overview

Dell Technologies is a global leader in IT infrastructure, operating through two primary segments:

  • Infrastructure Solutions Group (ISG): Servers, storage, networking – the primary growth engine driven by AI-optimized servers (e.g., PowerEdge with NVDA GPUs). Revenue share ~55%.

  • Client Solutions Group (CSG): PCs, laptops, peripherals – a mature but cash-generative business benefiting from the Windows 11 refresh cycle. Revenue share ~45%.

  • Competitive Position: Dell holds #1 or #2 market share in servers, storage, and PCs globally. Its direct sales model and supply chain scale provide a cost advantage over peers like HPQ and HPE.

📊 Financial Analysis

  • Revenue: FY2026 revenue of $113.5B (+18.8% YoY from $95.6B in FY2025), driven by ISG growth. FY2024 revenue was $88.4B.

  • Margins: Gross profit rose to $22.7B (20.0% margin) vs $21.3B (19.8%) in FY2025. Operating income grew to $8.15B (7.2% margin) from $6.24B (6.5%). SG&A as a % of revenue declined to 10.1% from 12.5%, showing cost discipline.

  • Profitability: Net income surged to $5.94B (diluted EPS $8.68) from $4.58B ($6.38) in FY2025, a 36% increase.

  • Cash Flow: Operating cash flow of $11.2B (FY2026) vs $4.5B (FY2025), driven by working capital improvements. CapEx was $2.6B, yielding FCF of ~$8.6B.

  • Balance Sheet: Total debt of $23.5B (long-term) vs cash of $11.5B, net debt of ~$12B. The company reduced debt by $8.5B in FY2026. Total equity turned positive to $1.2B (from -$1.4B in FY2025) due to retained earnings growth.

  • Justification: The premium is partially justified by 30%+ earnings growth and the AI server tailwind, but it leaves little room for error. A re-rating to 35x P/E would imply a $304 stock price, suggesting downside risk if growth slows.
