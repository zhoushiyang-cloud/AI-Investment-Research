ℹ️ Business Overview

Cognex Corporation is the global leader in machine vision systems, providing image-based barcode readers and vision sensors/software used in factory automation, logistics, and semiconductor inspection. The company competes primarily against KEYENCE (private), BASLER, and in-house automation solutions from NVDA-powered AI vision stacks.

Key end-market exposure (estimated):

  • Logistics & e-commerce (~35-40% of revenue): Parcel sorting, warehouse automation — highly cyclical but recovering.

  • Automotive & EV manufacturing (~20-25%): Battery inspection and assembly verification.

  • Semiconductor & electronics (~20%): Wafer inspection and precision alignment — benefiting from AI capex cycle.

  • Consumer goods & food/beverage (~15%): Quality control and traceability.

Competitive moat: Proprietary deep-learning vision software (ViDi suite), ~$386M goodwill/intangibles from strategic tuck-ins, and an installed base that creates high switching costs. However, the company faces increasing competition from lower-cost Asian vendors and open-source AI vision models.
