  • Nebius Group (NBIS) is a pure-play AI infrastructure company, operating an AI-native cloud platform with GPU clusters, data solutions (Toloka AI), edtech (TripleTen), and autonomous driving (Avride). It was formerly Yandex N.V., rebranded in August 2024.

ℹ️ Business Overview

Nebius Group N.V. is a technology company building comprehensive infrastructure for the global AI industry. Following its rebranding from Yandex N.V. in August 2024, the company has pivoted entirely toward AI infrastructure.

Key Segments:

  • Nebius (AI Cloud Platform) — Core business: end-to-end AI infrastructure with GPU computing clusters, cloud platforms, and developer tools. This is the primary growth engine, driving the FY2025 revenue surge.

  • Toloka AI — Data solutions provider for generative AI development (data labeling, RLHF, etc.).

  • TripleTen — EdTech venture focused on tech career training.

  • Avride — Autonomous driving technology for self-driving vehicles and delivery robots.

Competitive Position: NBIS is positioning itself as a specialized, AI-first cloud alternative to general-purpose clouds. Its competitive advantage lies in deep expertise in GPU cluster management and AI workload optimization. The company maintains R&D facilities across Europe, North America, and Israel, with 1,543 full-time employees. The CEO is Arkady Volozh, the founder of Yandex. The company trades on NASDAQ with a beta of 1.44, indicating high sensitivity to tech-sector movements.

📊 Financial Analysis

Revenue Trends:

  • FY2025 revenue: $529.8M (vs. $117.5M in FY2024) — a 350% YoY growth rate.

  • FY2023 revenue was $8.92B (legacy Yandex operations); the current figures reflect the post-divestiture, pure-play AI infrastructure business.

Margins:

  • FY2025 gross profit: $363.6M (68.6% gross margin), up from $44.1M (37.5% margin) in FY2024 — significant operating leverage as the GPU cloud scales.

  • FY2025 operating loss: -$611.7M (vs. -$440.7M in FY2024), reflecting heavy investment in R&D ($177.3M) and SG&A ($380.1M).

  • FY2025 net income: $82.5M (positive, driven by $112.4M in other income and tax benefits), though this is not indicative of core operating profitability.

Cash Flow & Balance Sheet:

  • FY2025 operating cash flow: $384.8M (positive, a major milestone).

  • FY2025 capex: $4.066B — massive investment in GPU infrastructure, funded by $4.16B in debt issuance.

  • Cash position: $3.68B in cash/equivalents at FY2025 end, plus $43.5M restricted cash.

  • Total assets: $12.43B; total equity: $4.59B.

  • The company carries $4.10B in long-term debt, a significant leverage increase to fund growth.

Key Takeaway: The financials show a company in aggressive build-out mode. Revenue growth is exceptional, gross margins are healthy, and operating cash flow has turned positive. However, the scale of capex ($4.07B in FY2025 alone) means the company is reliant on continued capital markets access or debt financing to sustain growth.

However, the risk/reward is asymmetric — any growth deceleration would cause significant multiple compression. ⚠️ Risks

  1. Execution Risk on GPU Infrastructure Build-Out #critical

    2. The company spent $4.07B on capex in FY2025. If GPU delivery timelines slip, utilization rates disappoint, or the infrastructure is underutilized, the massive depreciation expense (projected to grow from $417.9M in FY2025) will crush margins. Any delay in bringing capacity online directly impacts revenue potential.

  1. Hyper-Competitive AI Cloud Market #critical

    2. NBIS competes directly with NVDA's cloud partners, AVGO-powered custom silicon clouds, and hyperscalers (AWS, Azure, GCP) with virtually unlimited capital. These players can subsidize AI cloud pricing, potentially forcing NBIS into margin compression or forcing it to differentiate on service quality alone.

  1. Customer Concentration & Churn Risk #medium

    2. As a relatively new entrant, revenue may be concentrated among a few large AI labs or enterprises. Loss of a single anchor tenant could materially impact FY2026 revenue. Insider selling activity (multiple transactions at $196-$273 range) may signal insider concerns about near-term valuation.

  1. Geopolitical & Regulatory Risk #medium

    2. Despite being Netherlands-headquartered, NBIS has Russian roots (formerly Yandex). Ongoing geopolitical tensions could lead to sanctions, regulatory scrutiny, or restrictions on technology exports, particularly around advanced AI chips.

  1. Leverage & Capital Markets Dependency #medium

    2. With $4.10B in long-term debt and a cash burn that requires continued heavy capex, NBIS is dependent on favorable capital markets conditions. A downturn in AI sentiment or rising interest rates could restrict access to capital, forcing a slowdown in growth plans.

  1. Key Person Risk #low

    2. CEO Arkady Volozh is the visionary founder. His departure or any legal/regulatory issues would be a significant negative for the stock.

  1. Potential Index Inclusion #medium-term

💬 Recommendation

Rating: HOLD with Medium conviction.

12-Month Price Target: $220.00

NBIS is a high-quality, well-capitalized player in the most exciting secular growth trend in technology. The FY2025 results demonstrate exceptional execution — 350% revenue growth and positive operating cash flow are remarkable achievements. The company has the balance sheet ($3.68B cash) to fund its aggressive expansion.

However, at ~92x revenue, the stock is priced for near-perfection. The recent 32% drawdown from highs suggests the market is becoming more discerning about AI infrastructure names. While the long-term opportunity is compelling, the risk/reward at current levels is balanced.
