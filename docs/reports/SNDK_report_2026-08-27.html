ℹ️ Business Overview

SanDisk Corporation designs, manufactures, and supplies NAND flash storage solutions, including solid-state drives (SSDs), embedded memory, removable cards, USB devices, and wafers/components. Headquartered in Milpitas, CA, with 11,100 employees, the company is led by CEO David V. Goeckeler.

Key segments (estimated revenue split based on product portfolio):

  • Enterprise SSDs & Data Center Solutions (~45-50% of revenue): The primary growth driver, benefiting from AI training/inference storage demands.

  • Embedded Memory Solutions (~25-30%): Mobile, automotive, and IoT applications.

  • Removable Memory & USB Devices (~15-20%): Consumer retail products with stable, lower-growth characteristics.

  • Wafers & Components (~5-10%): OEM supply to other memory manufacturers.

Competitive Position: SNDK competes directly with MU (Micron), SNDK's former parent WDC (Western Digital), and STX (Seagate) in storage. The company holds a top-tier position in NAND flash technology with advanced 3D NAND architecture. The AI-driven demand for high-bandwidth, high-capacity storage has created a supply-demand imbalance favoring NAND producers through 2026-2027. The company's vertical integration from wafer to finished module provides cost advantages.
