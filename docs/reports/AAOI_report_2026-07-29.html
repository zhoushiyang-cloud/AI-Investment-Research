ℹ️ Business Overview

  • Company: Applied Optoelectronics, Inc. (AAOI) is a vertically integrated designer and manufacturer of optical networking components, modules, and equipment. Headquartered in Sugar Land, Texas, AAOI operates across three segments:

  1. Data Center: The core growth engine. High-speed optical transceivers (100G/200G/400G/800G/1.6T) connecting servers, switches, and GPUs inside hyperscale and AI data centers. This is where the AI thesis lives.

  2. CATV (Cable TV): Broadband access equipment for cable operators (legacy, slow-growth).

  3. Telecom: Optical networking gear for telecommunications providers.

  • Competitive Position: AAOI is a niche player in the optical transceiver market, dwarfed by LITE and COHR. Its primary advantage is vertical integration (in-house laser design + manufacturing) and a US-based production footprint (Texas), which is increasingly important for supply-chain security. However, it lacks the scale and balance-sheet strength of larger rivals.

  • Revenue Drivers: AI data center buildout → demand for 800G/1.6T transceivers. The Texas factory expansion (announced July 14, 2026) signals management's confidence in multi-year demand. Key customers likely include hyperscalers like AMZN (AWS), MSFT (Azure), and GOOGL (GCP).
