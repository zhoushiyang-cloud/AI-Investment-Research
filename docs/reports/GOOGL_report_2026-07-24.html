ℹ️ Business Overview

Alphabet (GOOGL) is a global technology conglomerate with three primary segments:

  • Google Services (Search, YouTube, Android, Chrome): The core advertising business, generating the vast majority of revenue. FY2025 revenue of ~$340B (estimated ~85% of total).

  • Google Cloud (GCP, Workspace): The fastest-growing segment, benefiting from enterprise AI adoption. FY2025 revenue of ~$43B (estimated ~11% of total).

  • Other Bets (Waymo, Verily, DeepMind): Early-stage, high-potential ventures. Revenue is immaterial (~$2B) but represents long-term optionality.

Competitive Position: GOOGL holds a dominant position in digital advertising (Search/YouTube) and is a top-3 player in cloud infrastructure, competing with AMZN (AWS) and MSFT (Azure). Its competitive moat is built on proprietary AI (Gemini), massive data scale, and a distribution monopoly via Android and Chrome.   5. Execution Risk in Other Bets #low

    6. Waymo and Verily require significant investment with uncertain timelines to profitability. While not material today, persistent losses could become a distraction.
