> [!info] Business Overview
> Allegro MicroSystems designs and sells analog and mixed-signal ICs focused on **motion control** and **energy efficiency**. The company is a subsidiary of [[Sanken Electric]].
> - **Product Lines:** Magnetic sensor ICs (position, speed, current sensing) – ~60% of revenue; Power management ICs (motor drivers, voltage regulators, LED drivers) – ~30%; Photonics & 3D sensing (LiDAR components) – ~10%.
> - **End Markets:** Automotive is the dominant end-market (~70% of sales), with industrial (~25%) and other (5%). Key applications include EV traction inverters, ADAS, and industrial motor control.
> - **Competitive Position:** ALGM holds a strong niche in high-reliability magnetic sensing for automotive safety (ISO 26262). Competitors include [[ST]] (Sensata), [[ONTO]], and [[SLAB]], but ALGM’s focus on automotive-grade mixed-signal gives it a moat in safety-critical applications.
> - **Distribution:** Multi-channel (direct sales, reps, distributors). Consignment inventory models are used with key OEMs.

> [!example] Financial Analysis
> - **Revenue Trends:** FY2024 revenue was $1.05B. FY2025 fell to $725M (-31% YoY). FY2026 (trailing twelve months) is $890M, a -15% decline, indicating a stabilization.
> - **Margins:** Gross margin has compressed from 55.0% in FY2024 to 44.3% in FY2025, and recovered slightly to 46.3% in FY2026. Operating margin swung from +18.7% (FY2024) to -2.7% (FY2025) to +2.1% (FY2026). The decline is due to under-absorption of fixed costs and inventory write-downs.
> - **Profitability:** GAAP net income was $152.9M in FY2024, then -$72.8M in FY2025, and -$14.7M in FY2026. Diluted EPS fell from $0.78 to -$0.39 to -$0.08.
> - **Cash Flow:** Operating cash flow was $181.7M (FY2024), $61.9M (FY2025), and $163.1M (FY2026). Capex was $124.8M, $40.0M, and $38.2M respectively. Free cash flow turned positive in FY2026 at ~$125M.
> - **Balance Sheet:** Cash & equivalents of $168.8M vs. total debt of $285.7M (long-term). Net debt of ~$117M. The company has $203.3M in goodwill and $238.7M in intangibles, representing ~31% of total assets.

> [!info] Valuation Assessment
> - **P/E:** Trailing P/E (on FY2024 EPS of $0.78) is ~60x. On FY2025/FY2026 losses, P/E is not meaningful. Forward consensus EPS estimates (FY2027) are ~$0.50-$0.70, implying a forward P/E of ~66x-93x.
> - **EV/EBITDA:** Enterprise Value is ~$8.7B (market cap $8.66B + net debt $117M). Trailing EBITDA (FY2026) is ~$85M (operating income + D&A). EV/EBITDA = ~102x. This is extremely high vs. peers like [[FORM]] (25x), [[SLAB]] (35x), and [[CRUS]] (15x).
> - **Price/Sales:** P/S of ~9.7x is above the semiconductor peer median of ~5x, but not unusual for a high-growth auto semi company.
> - **Justification:** The premium is only justified if ALGM returns to FY2024 revenue levels ($1.05B) and 20%+ operating margins. At that level, EPS would be ~$1.50, giving a 31x P/E – still expensive but more defensible. Current valuation embeds a recovery that has not yet materialized.

> [!warning] Risks
> 1. **Automotive Cycle Downturn** `#critical`
>    ALGM derives ~70% of revenue from automotive. A prolonged global auto production slowdown (especially in EVs) could push revenue below $700M, leading to further operating losses. FY2025 revenue fell 31% – a repeat would be devastating.
> 2. **Customer Concentration & Inventory Destocking** `#critical`
>    The company relies on a few large OEMs and Tier-1 suppliers. Consignment inventory models mean ALGM bears the risk of customer inventory corrections. In FY2025, inventory rose to $183.9M, and the company took impairment charges.
> 3. **Execution Risk in LiDAR/Photonics** `#medium`
>    ALGM’s photonics segment (LiDAR) is nascent and competes with [[NVDA]]-backed solutions and other optical sensor companies. If LiDAR adoption in automotive slows, this growth driver may fail to materialize.
> 4. **Debt & Interest Expense** `#medium`
>    Long-term debt of $285.7M is manageable, but interest expense was $30.4M in FY2025 (vs. $10.8M in FY2024). Rising rates or further debt issuance could pressure earnings.
> 5. **Parent Company Influence** `#low`
>    As a subsidiary of [[Sanken Electric]], strategic decisions (e.g., dividends, M&A) may not always align with minority shareholders’ interests.

> [!tip] Catalysts
> 1. **Automotive Inventory Normalization** `#near-term`
>    If the automotive destocking cycle ends in H2 2025, ALGM could see a sharp revenue rebound to $1B+ run-rate. The FY2026 revenue of $890M suggests the trough may be behind.
> 2. **EV/ADAS Content Growth** `#medium-term`
>    ALGM’s magnetic sensors are critical for EV traction inverters and ADAS (e.g., steering, braking). As EV penetration rises from ~20% to 30%+ by 2027, ALGM’s content per vehicle could increase 2-3x.
> 3. **New Product Cycles (LiDAR)** `#medium-term`
>    The company’s photonic ICs for LiDAR (used in autonomous driving) could become a meaningful revenue contributor if OEMs adopt solid-state LiDAR in 2026-2027.
> 4. **Margin Recovery** `#near-term`
>    With fixed cost absorption improving, gross margins could return to 50%+ in FY2027, driving a significant EPS swing from -$0.08 to $0.60+.

> [!quote] Recommendation
> **HOLD** – Conviction: Low
> **Price Target:** $50 (12-month forward)

The balance sheet is solid, and the company is a key supplier in secular growth trends (EV, ADAS). However, near-term earnings are negative, and the valuation (60x+ forward P/E) leaves no room for error. We recommend waiting for a clearer demand inflection or a pullback to $35-40 before accumulating.
