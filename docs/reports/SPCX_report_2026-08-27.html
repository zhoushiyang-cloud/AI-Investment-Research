ℹ️ Business Overview

Space Exploration Technologies Corp. (SPCX) is a vertically integrated aerospace and defense company founded in 2002 by Elon Musk. It operates two primary, highly synergistic segments:

  1. Launch Services (estimated ~30-40% of revenue, data not available): Provides commercial, civil, and national security launch services using Falcon 9 and Falcon Heavy. The reusable architecture provides a significant cost advantage over competitors. The company is the primary launch provider for NASA (ISS crew/cargo) and the US military.

  2. Starlink (estimated ~60-70% of revenue, data not available): A satellite-based broadband internet constellation serving consumer, commercial, and government (Starshield) customers. This is the primary growth engine, generating recurring subscription revenue.

Competitive Position: SPCX holds a dominant, defensible position. It has effectively commoditized launch through reusability, leaving competitors like LMT, BA, and international players (Arianespace, Roscosmos) at a structural disadvantage. Starlink has a first-mover scale advantage with over 22,000 employees supporting its operations. The company serves government agencies, national security organizations, commercial satellite operators, and research institutions. The Starship program is the key long-term catalyst, designed to reduce launch costs further and enable missions to the Moon and Mars.
