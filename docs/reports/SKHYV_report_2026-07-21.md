> 1. **Memory Price Cycle Downturn** `#critical`
>    DRAM and NAND prices are highly cyclical. A glut in supply or a slowdown in demand (e.g., from a recession or AI capex pullback) could erase 50-70% of earnings, consistent with the stock's beta of **2.027**.
> 2. **Geopolitical / Supply Chain Risk** `#critical`
>    SK hynix is headquartered in Icheon, South Korea. Any escalation in US-China tensions, particularly regarding semiconductor export controls, could disrupt its manufacturing base or limit sales to key markets like China.
> 3. **Technology Transition Risk** `#medium`
>    The race for HBM4 and next-gen NAND is capital-intensive. A failure to maintain leadership against [[Samsung]] or [[Micron]] could lead to rapid market share loss and margin compression.
> 4. **Customer Concentration** `#medium`
>    A significant portion of HBM revenue is tied to a single customer, [[NVDA]]. Any shift in [[NVDA]]'s supplier diversification strategy (e.g., increasing orders from [[Micron]] or [[Samsung]]) would be a major negative catalyst.
