> - **Cloud Services and License Support** (~75% of total revenue): Includes Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI), autonomous database, SaaS applications (Fusion, NetSuite), and license support.
> - **Cloud License and On-Premise License** (~10%): Perpetual software licenses.
> - **Hardware and Services** (~15%): Server, storage, and consulting services.
> 
> **Competitive Position**: ORCL is the #2 cloud infrastructure provider behind [[AMZN]] AWS and competes with [[MSFT]] Azure and [[GOOGL]] GCP. Its database franchise (Oracle DB) remains the market leader in relational databases, but faces pressure from open-source alternatives (PostgreSQL) and cloud-native databases. The company has aggressively expanded its data center footprint, with gross PPE nearly doubling from $34.8B in FY2024 to $122.7B in FY2026, signaling a massive bet on AI/cloud infrastructure.
