> **Key Revenue Segments (FY2025):**
> - **Government (U.S. + Allied):** ~55-60% of revenue. Driven by long-term contracts with the DoD, CIA, and allied nations. High visibility, multi-year deals.

In commercial, it competes with [[MSFT]] (Azure/AI), [[SNOW]] (data cloud), and custom AI solutions from [[NVDA]]-powered startups, but Palantir’s advantage lies in its ontology layer and ability to operationalize AI on messy, real-world data.
