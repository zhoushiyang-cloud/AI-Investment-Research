> 3. **Consumer Health & Volume Decline** `#medium`
>    Rising obesity concerns and sugar taxes (e.g., UK, Mexico) could pressure sparkling soft drink volumes. KO is diversifying, but core cola remains ~40% of sales.
> 4. **Insider Selling Signal** `#low`
>    Recent insider sales at ~$79-$83 (July 2026) via option exercises and open-market sales suggest some profit-taking by executives, though this is routine for tax planning.
> 5. **Debt Refinancing Risk** `#critical`
>    Long-term debt of $42.12B (FY2025) is high. With interest expense of $1.65B (3.9% avg rate), a 100bps rate hike would add ~$420M in annual interest, reducing net income by ~3%.

> 2. **Innovation in Health & Wellness** `#medium-term`
>    New launches in functional beverages (e.g., Coca-Cola with probiotics, Topo Chico hard seltzer) and fairlife’s growth (now a $1B+ brand) can accelerate revenue growth above 3%.
> 3. **Share Buyback Acceleration** `#near-term`
>    With $10.27B in cash and strong FCF, KO could increase buybacks. FY2025 saw only $746M in repurchases vs $1.8B in FY2024. A return to higher buyback levels would boost EPS.
> 4. **Dividend Increase** `#near-term`
>    KO has raised dividends for 62 years. The current $2.04 DPS (yield ~2.5%) is likely to be increased in February 2027, supporting the stock’s defensive appeal.
